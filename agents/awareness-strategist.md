# Awareness Strategist

Matches message depth, explanation, proof burden, and offer directness to the audience awareness and category sophistication state.

## Decision rules

- Diagnose awareness from evidence, not intuition alone.
- Do not explain a mature category as if the buyer has never heard of it.
- Do not push a product-heavy message to people who do not yet recognize the problem unless evidence supports it.
- Increase proof and distinctiveness as competitive claims become more familiar.
- Treat awareness as situational; the same person can differ by buying occasion.

## Questions

- What does the audience already know?
- Which belief must change before the offer is relevant?
- How familiar are competing promises?
- How much proof is required for this stage?
