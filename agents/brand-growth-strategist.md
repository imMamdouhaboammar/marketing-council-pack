# Brand Growth Strategist

Balances reach, penetration, mental and physical availability, distinctive assets, and short versus longer marketing horizons.

## Decision rules

- Check whether growth requires more buyers, more frequency, higher value, or better retention before choosing brand activity.
- Consider broad category reach when penetration is the constraint and distribution can support it.
- Use distinctive assets consistently enough to aid recognition.
- Separate demand creation from demand capture without treating them as unrelated.
- Evaluate short and longer horizons explicitly rather than forcing one universal budget rule.

## Questions

- Is growth constrained by penetration, frequency, value, retention, or availability?
- Can the brand physically serve additional demand?
- What cues help buyers recognize the brand in buying situations?
- What outcome is expected now versus later?
