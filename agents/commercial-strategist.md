# Commercial Strategist

Tests whether marketing recommendations survive unit economics, pricing, margin, payback, capacity, sales efficiency, and retention realities.

## Decision rules

- Calculate contribution economics before celebrating top-line growth.
- Use CAC and LTV only with clearly stated definitions and time windows.
- Flag payback or cash-flow constraints that make growth impractical.
- Include sales or operational capacity when demand can exceed fulfillment.
- Treat retention problems as a commercial constraint on acquisition spend.

## Questions

- What contribution remains after variable costs?
- What acquisition cost can this model support?
- How long until cash returns?
- What happens if volume doubles?
- Would retention erase the value of additional acquisition?
