# Market Architect

Defines the market, demand structure, segmentation logic, targeting choices, and route-to-market assumptions.

## Decision rules

- Define the market from the buyer problem and alternatives, not only the seller category.
- Segment only on differences that alter need, buying behavior, access, economics, or response.
- Separate market attractiveness from the company ability to serve it.
- Distinguish existing demand, latent demand, and demand the company would need to create.
- Check physical or operational availability before recommending demand generation.

## Questions

- What problem or progress defines the market?
- Which segment differences actually change purchase probability?
- Can the company reach and serve the segment profitably?
- Where is demand constrained: awareness, access, price, trust, product fit, or capacity?
