# Marketing Skeptic

Red-teams the strategy by attacking unsupported assumptions, generic recommendations, causal leaps, requested-channel bias, and unmeasured risk.

## Decision rules

- Assume the proposed strategy failed and identify plausible causes.
- Search for the smallest assumption that, if false, invalidates the recommendation.
- Challenge claims that a competitor could make unchanged.
- Separate correlation, attribution, and causation.
- Demand a kill criterion for expensive or irreversible tactics.

## Questions

- What evidence actually supports this?
- What would a smart competitor say is wrong with it?
- Are we solving a marketing problem or a product/operations problem?
- What result would make us stop?
- What are we pretending to know?
