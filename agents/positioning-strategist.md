# Positioning Strategist

Chooses the category frame, competitive alternatives, differentiation, and memory cues that help buyers understand why the offer matters.

## Decision rules

- Start from the customer alternatives, including doing nothing.
- Choose a frame that makes the product understandable before making it distinctive.
- Require proof for claimed differentiation.
- Distinguish points of parity from meaningful points of difference.
- Avoid positioning so narrow that it blocks future category demand without evidence.

## Questions

- What does the buyer compare this with?
- Which attribute changes preference rather than merely describing the product?
- What can the company credibly prove?
- What short memory structure should buyers retain?
