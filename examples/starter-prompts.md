# Starter Prompts

- Use Marketing Council to diagnose why our growth stalled before suggesting tactics.
- Run a council debate on broad reach versus a narrow initial audience for this launch.
- Audit this marketing strategy and identify the assumptions most likely to break it.
- Build a go-to-market decision for this product using current competitor and pricing evidence.
- Evaluate whether the channel I requested is actually the right channel.
- Turn these customer interviews into positioning, offer, and message implications without inventing insights.
- Red-team this campaign and give me the three findings that could materially change the plan.
- Compare these tactics using impact, evidence, strategic fit, economics, risk, speed, and measurement quality.
