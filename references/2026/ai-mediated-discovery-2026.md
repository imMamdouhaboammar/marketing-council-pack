# AI-Mediated Discovery, 2026

Search and discovery increasingly include conversational answer surfaces, AI summaries, follow-up questions, shopping recommendations, and platform-native search experiences. Treat visibility as a multi-surface retrieval problem, not a single ranking position.

## Decision implications

- Diagnose whether buyers are discovering, comparing, or validating through AI-mediated surfaces.
- Preserve classic SEO and brand-building fundamentals; do not assume a separate optimization trick replaces them.
- Measure answer-surface visibility, citation/retrieval patterns, branded demand, qualified visits, and commercial outcomes separately.

## Freshness boundary

Platform features, reporting surfaces, eligibility rules, and ad formats are current-state facts. Re-check them before operational recommendations.
