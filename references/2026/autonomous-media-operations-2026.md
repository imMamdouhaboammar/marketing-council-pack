# Autonomous Media Operations, 2026

Ad platforms are increasing AI-assisted campaign creation, recommendations, audience discovery, creative analysis, budgeting, and execution. The marketer's job shifts toward signal design, objectives, constraints, review rights, and exception handling.

## Decision implications

- Define what the platform may decide, what it may explore, and what requires approval.
- Feed business-quality outcomes rather than convenient but noisy proxy conversions.
- Require rollback conditions and audit trails for material automated changes.

## Freshness boundary

Available agents, APIs, MCP interfaces, bidding controls, and campaign automation vary by platform and account. Verify before execution.
