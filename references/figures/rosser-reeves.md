# Rosser Reeves

## Contribution

A decision card for the Direct Response & Scientific Advertising school. Use the principles, not the personality.

## Principles

- a proposition needs a clear customer benefit.
- distinctiveness matters when competitors cannot credibly make the same promise.
- repetition works better when the core claim is stable.

## Apply when

- The current decision matches this figure's documented area of contribution.
- The principle can be tied to evidence, a mechanism, or a testable assumption.

## Do not infer

- The figure's name does not settle a disagreement.
- Historical work is not evidence that a current market fact is true.
- Do not mimic personal voice, mannerisms, or biography.
