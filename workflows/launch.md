# Launch Workflow

1. Verify product, inventory/service, onboarding, sales, and support readiness.
2. Define the initial buying situation and reachable audience.
3. Confirm category frame, product truth, proof, and offer.
4. Choose launch channels by job and operational fit.
5. Sequence testable launch moments before irreversible scale.
6. Define leading signals, contribution outcomes, retention guardrails, and stop rules.
